  • Trailcam Downloader

    Automated SD card downloader for trail cameras. Continuously monitors for SD card insertions, automatically downloads media files to date-organized folders, erases the card, and safely ejects it.

    Source

  • Rawesome

    A browser-based RAW photo editor. GPU-accelerated via WebGL2 with support for CR2, NEF, ARW, DNG, RAF, ORF & more. Non-destructive edits — exposure, white balance, contrast, clarity, HSL, crop & film simulations — with no uploads, no account & no photos leaving your machine.

    Project Source

  • Tiny QR

    Enter a URL, generate a QR code, and print it directly to a Niimbot thermal label printer over Bluetooth — no drivers, no app, just the browser.

    Project Source

  • Capturer

    A regular expression tool that uses capture groups to isolate content. Built in React & uses web workers to keep the UI quick.
